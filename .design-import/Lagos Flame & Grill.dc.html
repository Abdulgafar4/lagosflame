Our story

FROM LAGOS TO BC.

In Lagos, suya is how the night smells. Smoke curling off a roadside grill, fat crackling over charcoal, yaji rubbed in by hand and served with raw onion and tomato. It's street food, and it's home.

We built this truck to carry that fire here — the same yaji, the same open flame, the same big-table energy — to Chilliwack and wherever British Columbia gathers.
